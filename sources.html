How we checked facts

  • We prefer institutional sources (UNESCO, the Archaeological Survey of India, the GI Registry, museums) and scholarly books over websites that summarise them.
  • Dates, numbers and attributions need two independent sources.
  • When good sources disagree, the entry is marked as disputed and we show the range or the cautious wording.
  • Every image lists its creator, licence and source link.
